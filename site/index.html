A few things to know

Room for
questions.

What do I need to use Koini?+

Koini is an iPhone app for iOS 26 or later, currently distributed through TestFlight. LiDAR room scanning requires a supported iPhone Pro; you can also enter a room’s dimensions and shape manually.

Can I use my own furniture?+

Yes. Koini supports personal furniture models alongside its catalogue. You can arrange them at their saved dimensions and explore how they work in your room.

How does room sharing work?+

In Rooms, open the room’s menu and choose “Share a copy…”. Sign in to create a link, then send it to another Koini user. They can save their own editable copy. Both users need a build containing room sharing.

Does a shared link update when I edit my room?+

A link shares a snapshot. Replace it to include later changes, or stop it at any time. Links expire after 30 days. Copies someone has already saved remain independent.
